## Who this is for

Anyone new to AI who wants to use chatbots and image generators safely. No technical background required.

Written plainly, step by step, like a friend would explain it. Warm, never condescending, no fear-mongering.

## Topics covered

1. **Data privacy** — keep your info yours
2. **Scams and deepfakes** — how to spot them
3. **What never to paste** — the short, memorable list
4. **Checklists** — before you paste, when you receive a surprise, after a session

## How to use

- Read the manual in `docs/manual.md`, or open `site/index.html` in your browser.
- Print `docs/checklists.md` and keep it handy.
- This is education only — not legal, medical, financial, or cybersecurity advice.
