## Checklist A: Before you paste anything into a chatbot

- [ ] I am not pasting passwords, API keys, or secret codes.
- [ ] I am not pasting bank, credit card, or payment details.
- [ ] I am not pasting government ID numbers (SSN, passport, etc.).
- [ ] I am not pasting medical records or health details about myself or others.
- [ ] I am not pasting private info about other people (friends, family, patients, customers, coworkers).
- [ ] I am not pasting employer documents that aren't public (contracts, payroll, internal memos, source code).
- [ ] If I need to share an example, I made up fake names and numbers instead.
- [ ] I checked the chatbot's privacy settings and turned off data training if available.

**Rule of thumb:** If you would not want a stranger to read it, do not paste it.

---

## Checklist B: When you receive a surprising message, call, or image

- [ ] I paused and did not act right away.
- [ ] I checked the sender's actual email address or phone number.
- [ ] I did not click any links or call any numbers in the message.
- [ ] For a voice call, I hung up and called the person back on a known number.
- [ ] For an image or video, I looked for unnatural eyes, mouth, lip-sync, or background.
- [ ] I checked a trusted news source or reverse-image search before believing shocking claims.
- [ ] If money was requested, I refused and talked to someone I trust.

**Rule of thumb:** Urgency is a red flag. Slow down.

---

## Checklist C: After you finish a chatbot session

- [ ] I deleted the chat history if the app allows it.
- [ ] I logged out if I was on a shared or public device.
- [ ] I reviewed what I pasted and confirmed nothing sensitive was included.
- [ ] If I pasted something sensitive by mistake, I changed the affected passwords and notified the right people.

---

## What never to paste (the short list)

- Passwords and login credentials
- API keys, secret keys, or access tokens
- Credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or payment info
- Social Security numbers or government ID numbers
- Your full home address or phone number
- Medical records, diagnoses, or test results (yours or anyone else's)
- Private info about other people
- Employer documents that aren't public
- Anything you would not want a stranger to read
